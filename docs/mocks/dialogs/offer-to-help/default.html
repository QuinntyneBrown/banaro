About Psalter

Psalter helps you hide Scripture in your heart without the guilt. Each day it shows the few verses that are about to slip away, and lets you rehearse them in under three minutes.

It began as a tool for Daniel's own family and is now used by 1,900 beta testers across about forty churches. The code is open source, and the next stretch of work is offline review, shared decks for small groups, and better accessibility.

Daniel is looking for contributors who enjoy small, well-tested pull requests: Laravel, Angular, or careful writing for the onboarding.
