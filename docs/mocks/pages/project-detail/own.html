About Harvest

Harvest takes the Tuesday-night spreadsheet out of food-bank volunteering. Coordinators publish shifts, volunteers pick the ones that suit them, and everyone gets a gentle reminder the day before.

It is in beta with six food banks across Toronto and the GTA. Amara is a product person looking for a technical co-founder who cares about the work.
