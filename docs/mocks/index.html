Banaro — 40 design concepts

The same two pages — the signed-out home page and the signed-in builder directory — designed forty different ways, from Swiss minimalism to WebGPU auroras. Hover a preview to see the directory. Every mock has a light and dark theme, works at phone width, and opens from disk.

40 concepts Cast, copy and conventions

Minimalism · whitespace · no photos

International Typographic Style: a strict 12-column grid, flush-left grotesque type, hairline rules, huge section numerals and a single signal-red accent. No photos, no illustrations — the grid is the decoration.
