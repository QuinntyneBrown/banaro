# Shared photography for the concept mocks

Stock photographs from Unsplash, used under the Unsplash Licence (free to use, no attribution
required), resized and recompressed for the mocks (scenes 1600 px, portraits 480 px square).
They stand in for photographs of real Banaro gatherings and members; production must use
consented photos of the community. Portraits are named after the fictional cast in
`docs/mocks/README.md`; the people pictured are not those builders.
