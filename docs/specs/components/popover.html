Popover

A non-modal panel anchored to a button, holding a title and a short explanation. No mock uses it yet; the builder-profile "More" list is a menu (D-1). Requirements: popover.md. Design system: popover.

Responsive

Layout does not change across breakpoints; the panel keeps inside the viewport. A trigger at the right edge aligns the panel to the trigger's end, and text wraps.

The perf-test scenario (AC-15) is measured, not drawn.
