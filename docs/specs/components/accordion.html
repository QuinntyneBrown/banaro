Sizes

One size: the summary is at least --target-comfortable (44 px) high and the item is as wide as its container (AC-17).

States

Hover, focus and active are forced with data-state on the summary; expanded uses the native open attribute. Hover and active have no visual change beyond the pointer cursor (D-11). There is no disabled state (D-4).

Each theme shows the items on a card surface, then directly on the page canvas. Closed and open differ by the marker’s direction and the shown content, not by colour. Accessible names and states come from the native elements (AC-9); the rendering page is an axe target (AC-10).

AC-9, AC-10, AC-12, AC-13, AC-14

Responsive

The layout does not change across breakpoints. The frames show the 44 px summary at 360 px and the longest French summary wrapping at 320 px with no horizontal scroll.

Motion

Nothing animates: items open and close in the same frame under both prefers-reduced-motion settings (D-12).

Performance

Scenarios: Accordion (one closed "Availability details" item), AccordionGroup (single-open group, "Availability details" open, "Skills" closed) and DarkTheme. The open state is server-rendered, so hydration never shifts content. All copy comes from the catalogue.
