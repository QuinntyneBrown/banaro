Principles

The system has four warm, soft shadows. Use level 1 for subtle separation, level 2 for resting cards, level 3 for hover and toasts, and level 4 for overlays.

Dark surfaces

Raised charcoal is lighter than the base surface. Stronger shadows support, rather than replace, that separation. Decorative lines may be low contrast; interactive boundaries may not.

Layers

Base and raised content sit below the sticky header. Dropdown, overlay, modal, popover, toast and tooltip layers follow in that order. Native top-layer dialogs and popovers outrank CSS stacking contexts; z-index cannot substitute for modality.
