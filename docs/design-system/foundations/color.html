Color

Principles

The surface palette is warm and quiet. Sage highlights the main decision; clay and fjord distinguish initials tiles. Roles, not literal values, decide which colours touch.

Role hierarchy

Canvas surrounds the page; surface holds cards and fields; raised surface carries dialogs and menus. Linen wells sit below the reading plane. In dark mode, charcoal surfaces become lighter as they rise. Disabled content is deliberately subdued and exempt from active-control contrast requirements.

Status

Info uses fjord, success uses sage, warning uses clay and danger uses lingon. Keep a word and icon beside each tone. Decorative hairline borders are not sufficient boundaries for a control; inputs and focus indicators use strong roles.
