Principles

Speak as a local builder inviting another person to build together. Faith is part of the community’s purpose; product copy stays concrete and welcoming.

Voice and casing

Use sentence case, short active sentences and specific nouns. Keep Directory, Projects, Events and Matching stable. Avoid exaggerated compatibility claims and urgency around relationship decisions.

Dates, numbers and distance

Use Toronto local time with America/Toronto conversion; include timezone context when scheduling. Show 1,284 builders, 40 km, Thu 15 Oct and 7:00–9:30 pm. Preserve machine-readable time values.

Error formula

Name the problem, say what remains safe, then give one correction: Message not sent. Your note is still here. Try again.

Empty formula

Explain what is absent and one way forward: No builders match yet. Try fewer skills or a wider distance. Clear filters.
